Welcome

I am a machine learning researcher building foundation models for health data. In my PhD at the University of Copenhagen and the Pioneer Centre for AI, I pretrain transformer models on the longitudinal health records of around 2.2 million patients from Danish nationwide registries, and use them to predict clinical outcomes, discover patient subgroups and explain risk.

Before moving to health records, I spent seven years developing deep learning methods for medical image analysis (MRI, ultrasound, retinal imaging and radiographs). I am now bringing both directions together in multimodal clinical AI, combining medical imaging with health records, as a guest researcher at DTU Compute.
